About Cyberpunk RED Generator (v5.0.0)

A fully self-contained, highly-optimised interactive character manager and Game Master command center for Cyberpunk RED by R. Talsorian Games. Create, customise, save, run GM encounters, and print characters with full support for stats, skills, role abilities, gear, cyberware, lifepath generation, and multiclassing.

Welcome to Version 5.0.0! This major release introduces the 🎲 Game Master (GM) Command Center, enabling GMs to load up to 5 exported character sheets side-by-side with dynamic column scaling, quick HP/SP steppers, full interactive sheet modals with d10 check and attack rolling, d6 damage rolling, and automatic session persistence across browser sessions.

v5.0.0 Key Features

  • 🎲 GM Command Center: Load up to 5 character sheets side-by-side with dynamic responsive scaling (1 to 5 columns).
  • ⚡ Interactive GM Popup Modal: Click any sheet card to open a tabbed modal featuring live Vitals & STAT editing, Combat attack & damage rolling, searchable Skills & Check rolls (+1d10 critical explosion/fumble calculation), Armor & Gear overview, and GM Encounter Notes.
  • 💾 Automatic Session Persistence: Active GM Screen sheets automatically persist to local storage across browser refreshes until cleared with 🗑️ Clear All.
  • 🔄 Live Sheet Re-syncing & Updates: Update individual character cards from fresh .json files or re-sync all cards against local storage while retaining GM session notes.
  • 💾 Session Export & Import: Export the entire active GM Screen into a single combined session file (Cyberpunk_GM_Session.json) to reload anytime.
  • 🎲 Quick GM Standalone Dice Roller: Fast d10, d6, and 3d6 roll dialog accessible anytime in the GM Screen.
  • Title-Cased Random Street Handles: Random handle generator produces clean, properly title-cased street names (e.g. Karma Tiger, Flux Fox, Creed Eagle).
  • Persistent In-Page DOM Modals: Catalog additions, slot options, item sales, and custom item creations use tab-persistent, dark-theme modals.
  • Editable Armor SP (Abrasion Tracking): Track combat damage on armor directly on the character sheet with dynamically recalculated Total Body & Head SP.
  • Looted & Purchased Quantity Management: Easily increase item quantities for free when looted in combat (0eb) or at listed EB cost.
  • Categorised Ammunition & Weapon Mapping: Select specific weapon types for ammo with equipped weapon dropdowns and combined EB cost fields.
  • Grenade & Explosive Bundles: Grenades bought and tracked in individual 1-unit bundles.
  • 10 Stats with point-buy system (62 points, min 2 / max 8)
  • All 10 Roles with detailed rank-by-rank ability descriptions and deeply integrated mechanical trackers
  • Interactive Role Abilities: Create and manage Exec Team Members, track Nomad Family Vehicles & Upgrades, handle Lawman Backup stats, manage Netrunner Interface abilities, and track Medtech Pharmaceuticals
  • Multiclass support - take a secondary role once your primary reaches Rank 4
  • 86 Skills grouped by linked stat with search, ranks, and item bonuses
  • 140+ Weapons mapped accurately to their Cyberpunk RED skills, including Black Chrome selections
  • JSON Export/Import for sharing characters or backing them up safely
  • Print-Friendly Layout: Smart Selective Printing with restacked DOM layout for perfect page rendering
  • Mobile-Responsive: Touch-optimized controls and seamless skill adjustments
  • Full Lifepath generation (Generic and Role-specific) with randomise button
  • Random character generator — one-click complete, playable character
